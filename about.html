How FinCoach works

A finance tutor built for people who have decided they are not math people. Spoiler: they usually are.

Why FinCoach exists

I tutor finance, and the same thing happens again and again. A smart student sits down with a page of formulas, decides they are not a math person, and quietly stops listening. The problem is rarely ability. It is the order of teaching, because most classes hand over the symbols first and the meaning later. FinCoach flips that order. Every lesson starts with an everyday story, like a lemonade stand, a coffee cart or a car loan, so the idea feels familiar before any math shows up. By the time the formula arrives, the student already understands what it is trying to say.

Who it is for

FinCoach is for MBA students, undergraduates and career switchers who feel anxious about the quantitative side of finance. It is also for anyone who has memorized equations for an exam without ever feeling what they mean. The chatbot in the corner of this page answers questions at any hour, so help is there at midnight before a quiz and not only during office hours. It sticks to the course concepts below and does not give personal investment, tax or legal advice.

Try it now

Open the chat bubble and ask something like: "What is NPV, explained with a coffee cart?" Then tell it which part still feels fuzzy and watch it try a different story.

How one lesson goes

  1. A short story. A lemonade stand or a coffee cart sets the scene.
  2. Plain English. What the idea means, with no symbols yet.
  3. The formula. Shown once the idea already makes sense.
  4. The numbers. A worked example, one small step at a time.
  5. A quick check. One question to see if it stuck.
